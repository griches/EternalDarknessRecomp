Eternal Darkness Recomp - Windows (64-bit)
==========================================

Eternal Darkness: Sanity's Requiem as a native Windows game. You need your own
image of the European GameCube disc (GEDP01), as an .iso or .gcm. The USA disc
is not supported.

1. Unzip this folder somewhere you can write to, such as Documents or a Games
   folder (not Program Files).
2. Put your disc image in the "dump" folder. Any file name works.
3. Double-click play.bat. If Windows says it protected your PC, click
   "More info", then "Run anyway". A black window stays open behind the game
   while it runs; that's normal.

The game's program comes ready-made in this folder; its graphics, sound and
music come from your disc. The first start takes a second or two longer while
it reads what it needs from the disc.

HD textures (recommended)
-------------------------
Download both, unpack them (7-Zip opens the .7z file), and put the two folders
in a folder called "textures" in this folder.

- Eternal Darkness HD Texture Pack by Zombie (DDS, 2.9 GB .7z, on Mega):
  https://mega.nz/file/6m5TQToI#v09Y_gYdtZ24WTT6BlfCeM5uvw1nRs3QozcYZkUVVqg
- PAL supplement, the European disc's screens, words and Tome inventory page
  the pack misses (DDS, 13.2 MB .zip):
  https://www.bouncingball.mobi/eternaldarkness/files/ED-PAL-supplement-dds.zip?v=3

Leave both folders as they are; the supplement wins where they overlap.
Options > Game > HD textures turns them on and off.

Saves, settings and logs stay in this folder (saves\, settings.ini,
ed-*.dolcard, logs\). To update, copy a new build over this folder: your
saves, settings, disc and textures stay. If something goes wrong, send the
files in saves\reports\ and logs\play.log with a few words on what you were
doing.

Source and licences: https://github.com/griches/EternalDarknessRecomp
(licenses\ holds every component's licence text).
